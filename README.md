# Core Java Practice Programs

This repository contains beginner-friendly Core Java practice programs divided into **Level 1** and **Level 2**.  
The programs cover topics like basic arithmetic operations, user input handling, type conversions, operator precedence, and mathematical formulas.

---


---

## 📘 Level 1 Practice Programs

1. **Age Calculator**
   - **Description:** Find the age of Harry if the birth year is 2000. Assume the current year is 2024.
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `Harry's age in 2024 is ___`

2. **Average Marks Calculator**
   - **Description:** Sam’s marks in Maths = 94, Physics = 95, Chemistry = 96. Find the average percent.
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `Sam’s average mark in PCM is ___`

3. **Distance Converter (Fixed Values)**
   - **Description:** Convert 10.8 km to miles (1 km = 1.6 miles).
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `The distance ___ km in miles is ___`

4. **Profit and Loss Calculator**
   - **Description:** Cost Price = ₹129, Selling Price = ₹191. Calculate profit and profit percentage.
   - **Formula:**  
     ```
     profit = sellingPrice - costPrice
     profitPercentage = (profit / costPrice) * 100
     ```
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:**  
     ```
     The Cost Price is INR ___ and Selling Price is INR ___
     The Profit is INR ___ and the Profit Percentage is ___
     ```

5. **Pen Distribution**
   - **Description:** Divide 14 pens among 3 students equally. Find per-student pens and remainder.
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `The Pen Per Student is ___ and the remaining pen not distributed is ___`

6. **Fee Discount (Fixed Values)**
   - **Description:** Fee = ₹125000, Discount = 10%. Calculate discount amount and final payable fee.
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `The discount amount is INR ___ and final discounted fee is INR ___`

7. **Volume of Earth**
   - **Description:** Volume of sphere = `(4/3) × π × r³`, radius = 6378 km. Display in km³ and miles³.
   - **I/P:** None  
   - **O/P:** `The volume of earth in cubic kilometers is ____ and cubic miles is ____`

8. **Kilometers to Miles Converter (User Input)**
   - **Description:** Convert a given distance (km) to miles.
   - **Formula:** miles = km / 1.6
   - **I/P:** `km`  
   - **O/P:** `The total miles is ___ mile for the given ___ km`

9. **Fee Discount (User Input)**
   - **Description:** Take fee and discount % from the user, then calculate final fee.
   - **I/P:** `fee`, `discountPercent`  
   - **O/P:** `The discount amount is INR ___ and final discounted fee is INR ___`

10. **Height Converter**
    - **Description:** Convert height in cm to feet and inches. (1 foot = 12 inches, 1 inch = 2.54 cm)
    - **I/P:** `height`  
    - **O/P:** `Your Height in cm is ___ while in feet is ___ and inches is ___`

11. **Basic Calculator**
    - **Description:** Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on two floating-point numbers.
    - **I/P:** `number1`, `number2`  
    - **O/P:** `The addition, subtraction, multiplication and division value of 2 numbers ___ and ___ is ___, ____, ____, and ___`

12. **Triangle Area Calculator**
    - **Description:** Find area in square inches and cm from base and height (1 in = 2.54 cm).
    - **I/P:** `base`, `height`  
    - **O/P:** `The Area of the triangle in sq in is ___ and sq cm is ___`

13. **Square Side Finder**
    - **Description:** Find the side length from perimeter (Perimeter = 4 × side).
    - **I/P:** `perimeter`  
    - **O/P:** `The length of the side is ___ whose perimeter is ____`

14. **Distance Converter (Feet to Yards & Miles)**
    - **Description:** Convert feet to yards and miles. (1 mile = 1760 yards, 1 yard = 3 feet)
    - **I/P:** `distanceInFeet`  
    - **O/P:** `The distance in yards is ___ while the distance in miles is ___`

15. **Purchase Price Calculator**
    - **Description:** Calculate total price from unit price and quantity.
    - **I/P:** `unitPrice`, `quantity`  
    - **O/P:** `The total purchase price is INR ___ if the quantity ___ and unit price is INR ___`

16. **Handshake Calculator**
    - **Description:** Find max possible handshakes among N students. Formula: `(n × (n - 1)) / 2`
    - **I/P:** `numberOfStudents`  
    - **O/P:** `The number of possible handshakes is ___`
